## Phase 6 (admin, support, finance and marketing consoles): done
Four staff windows, each with its own sign-in (a support login is refused in the admin window; being signed in to one does not sign you in to another).
- **Admin:** overview, people (search, roles given and removed, disable and re-enable with a reason: sessions end, last admin protected), sellers (search, status, verification tier, commission override, team, history, internal notes), review queue for restricted-category products (approve, or remove with a reason), settings and feature flags (checked, audited), audit log with filters.
- **Support:** find an order by number, name or email; see the buyer and the whole order (payments, refunds, emails sent); internal notes that sellers and buyers never see; resend the confirmation; people lookup; audit log. Support cannot refund or pay.
- **Finance:** ledger overview (cash, owed to sellers, commission, GST), seller balances (earned, available, on hold, paid out), recording a bank-transfer payout (a balanced ledger entry; cannot exceed what has passed the hold after delivery, even with two clicks at once), order lookup and refunds on any order (same exact tax and commission shares), GST for any period, sales CSV export (spreadsheet formulas neutralised, every download audited).
- **Marketing:** home page curation (phone and desktop "New from makers" picks in order, category tiles, maker spotlight, the bar above the header) and search synonyms.
Verified: API **91 tests** (12 new for consoles: disabling people, suspending sellers and its effect on the shop, commission override, window separation, the review queue, support lookup and notes, payouts including the hold and parallel payouts, GST and CSV against the ledger, finance refunds, home curation with its checks, synonyms). `apps/e2e/console-flow.mjs` drives a real browser through **44 checks** across all four windows. ## Phase 7 (engagement): done
- **Reviews:** only someone who received a piece can review it, once per order (the private order link is the proof, so guests can too). Names are shortened ("Bea B."), the shop's displayed rating blends its base (the demo seed, zero for real shops) with its published reviews, reviews and replies show on product pages and the shop's Reviews tab. Makers reply once in public and can report a review with a reason; staff keep it or hide it (hidden reviews leave the rating); every step is audited. Delivering an order sends one "how was it?" email.
- **Messages:** a signed-in buyer can ask a maker about a piece; anyone with an order link (guests too) can message the maker about that order. Makers answer in the studio inbox with unread counts, the studio home counts waiting buyers, buyers have a messages list with unread dots. Email notices are throttled to one pending email per conversation, there is a 30-an-hour limit per side, makers never see a buyer's email, support can read an order's conversation (read only) to settle disputes, and the `order_chat` and `reviews` feature flags switch each off.
- **Saved page** now also shows the shops you follow with their newest pieces.
Verified: API **98 tests** (7 new: delivery and one-review rules, name shortening, rating blending, reports and moderation, review emails, flags, conversations and their isolation, email throttling, rate limit, own-shop block, guests' order chat, support visibility, following). `apps/e2e/engagement-flow.mjs`: **27 checks** in a real browser across buyer, maker, admin and support. Earlier suites pass: 23 visual screens, buyer (25), studio (38), consoles (44). Found by the new flow and fixed: the shop page and product page crashed or printed "null" for shops with no tagline or city (every new shop starts that way).
Not done: photo reviews, review helpfulness votes, buyer-to-buyer anything, push or SMS notifications, message attachments, blocking a user from messaging, automatic spam or abuse detection (only the rate limit and the report path for reviews), price-drop alerts for saved pieces.

## Phase 8 (ads): done
Sponsored placement, paid per click out of the seller's own earnings.
- **Sellers** (studio, Ads): campaigns with a name, the pieces to promote, a most-they-will-pay bid, a daily budget and an optional total budget; optional targeting by category and by search words (empty = the categories each piece belongs to, and searches that match it). Pause, resume, end. Numbers: views, clicks, click rate, spend, today against the daily budget, 14 days. Each campaign says in plain words why it is not running (paused, budget reached, stopped by staff, not enough earnings).
- **Shoppers** see up to two pieces marked "Sponsored" in category and search results, in fixed slots (after the 2nd and 7th result); the organic order is otherwise untouched and a sponsored piece is not shown twice.
- **The auction:** highest bid wins, one ad per seller per page, the winner pays one cent over the next bid down (never below the platform minimum, never above its own bid). Only pieces that are live, in stock, outside restricted categories, from an active seller who has earnings to pay for the click, within both budgets, and matching the page qualify.
- **Money:** a charged click is one balanced ledger entry (seller payable down, new `ad_revenue` account up). Clicks use a signed, single-use token that carries the price; tampered, replayed or expired tokens do nothing; the seller's own team and a repeat click from the same address within 30 minutes are free and not counted. Finance shows ad revenue; the whole ledger still sums to zero.
- **Staff** (marketing, Ads): see every campaign, stop one that breaks the rules with a reason (the seller sees it and cannot restart it) and release it later. Admin settings control the minimum bid and the number of slots; the `ads` feature flag switches it all off.
Verified: API **107 tests** (9 new: validation and ownership, restricted and unpublished pieces refused, eligibility, search and category matching, one-per-seller, slot setting, second-price pricing, single-use and tampered tokens, balanced ledger, repeat and own-team clicks, daily budget stop, empty balance, staff stop and release, finance ad revenue, feature flag). `apps/e2e/ads-flow.mjs`: **18 checks** in a real browser (two competing sellers, marked cards, order and slot, search ads, a $0.61 charged click against a 60-cent runner-up, a repeat click filtered, pause, staff stop with the seller seeing the reason, release). Earlier suites pass in order on a fresh seed: 23 visual screens, buyer (25), studio (38), consoles (44), engagement (27).
Not done: charging by view, conversion tracking and cost per sale, product-level quality scoring (the auction is bid only), ad creative beyond the product card, top-up wallets and invoices for ad spend (spend comes out of earnings), real-time fraud scoring beyond single-use tokens, the repeat-address rule and the own-team rule, scheduling campaigns by date.
